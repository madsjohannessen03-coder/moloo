How do I join my household?

Choose Join a household on the access screen and sign in. Accept your invitation or use the household’s invite code or QR code. An active household purchase covers up to seven members, including the owner.

Who can see my chores?

Everyone in your household can see chores in Shared, who has taken them and when they were finished. Use Personal for chores you want to keep to yourself.

How do I change my reminders?

Choose the reminders you want in Moloo. To change how they appear on your iPhone, open Settings, choose Notifications, then Moloo.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Use Restore purchases in Moloo while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy access. To turn off annual renewal, open Apple’s subscription settings. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Open Delete account in Moloo’s account settings and confirm your request. The app shows when the request was received. The target for completing deletion is one month. If you have an annual subscription, manage its renewal separately with Apple.
