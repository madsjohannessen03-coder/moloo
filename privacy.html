Moloo privacy policy

Shared chores are visible to your household. Personal chores and their completion history are visible only to you in the app.

28 November 2026

Who handles your information

Mads Johannessen, Norway, is the data controller for Moloo. For questions about how Moloo handles your information or to exercise your rights, email support@mjappdev.com.

Your account and household

Moloo uses an account identifier, your sign-in provider’s identifier and the name or email supplied during sign-in. We store the profile name and avatar you choose, household membership, invitations and any username you make searchable so people can invite you. Share your household invite link or code only with people you want to invite.

Inviting your child

Where child invitations are available, you must be at least 18 and own a paid Moloo household to invite your own child aged 13–17. When you start the adult check, Moloo sends your confirmed email address, your child’s country of residence, your selected language and a random invitation reference to Epic’s Kids Web Services (KWS). KWS emails you and checks that you are an adult. If the check succeeds, KWS sends Moloo a signed result with your email address and references that let us match it to your invitation. Your verification documents stay outside Moloo. Your child’s name and tasks stay outside KWS.

KWS keeps a hashed version of your email address and verification details so it can recognise a previous check. Its confirmation email includes a link to request removal from that record. KWS privacy policy.

After the check, Moloo asks you to confirm that you are the child’s parent or legal guardian and give permission for the child to join. We record your decision, when you gave it, which notice you saw, and a digital fingerprint of the verification receipt alongside your account and household. You can stop before giving permission. If you agree, Moloo gives you a single-use code valid for up to 30 minutes. An unused invitation expires after 24 hours. Moloo routinely removes unused invitation records more than seven days old.

If you’re joining as a child

Use the code from your parent or guardian. You can join without giving Moloo an email address or password. People in your household can see your profile name and the shared chores you complete. Only you can see your personal chores and their history in the app.

Your parent or guardian can open Children’s access and choose Withdraw. This closes your access and starts deletion of your managed account. You or your parent can ask about your information, request a correction or request deletion at support@mjappdev.com.

På norsk · Personvern for barn og foresatte

Der barneinvitasjoner er tilgjengelige, kan du som har fylt 18 år og eier en betalt husstand, invitere ditt eget barn på 13–17 år. Når du starter voksenkontrollen, sender Moloo din bekreftede e-postadresse, barnets bostedsland, valgt språk og en tilfeldig invitasjonsreferanse til Epic-tjenesten Kids Web Services (KWS). KWS sender deg en e-post og sjekker at du er voksen. Etter godkjent kontroll får Moloo en signert bekreftelse med e-postadressen din og referanser som knytter svaret til invitasjonen. Dokumentene du bruker til kontrollen, blir hos verifiseringstjenesten. Barnets navn og oppgaver deles ikke med KWS.

KWS lagrer et digitalt avtrykk av e-postadressen din og opplysninger om kontrollen for å kunne gjenkjenne deg senere. Du kan be om å bli fjernet fra dette registeret via lenken i bekreftelsen fra KWS. Personvern hos KWS.

Deretter spør Moloo om du har foreldreansvar og samtykker til at barnet blir med. Du kan stoppe her. Hvis du samtykker, registrerer Moloo valget, tidspunktet, versjonen av informasjonen og et digitalt avtrykk av kvitteringen knyttet til kontoen og husstanden din. Du får en engangskode som gjelder i opptil 30 minutter. En ubrukt invitasjon utløper etter 24 timer. Moloo rydder jevnlig bort ubrukte invitasjoner som er over sju dager gamle.

Til deg som er barn. Bruk koden fra den foresatte. Du trenger ingen e-postadresse eller passord. Familien ser profilnavnet ditt og hvilke fellesoppgaver du gjør. Bare du ser dine personlige oppgaver og historikken deres i appen.

Den foresatte kan velge Barnas tilgang og deretter Trekk tilbake. Da stenges barnets tilgang, og Moloo starter sletting av barnekontoen. Barn og foresatte kan be om innsyn, retting eller sletting på support@mjappdev.com.

Tasks and progress

We store task names, schedules, assignments, completion times and who completed a shared task. This gives your household a common calendar, a Clean score and a streak. Assigning a shared task to one person keeps it visible to the household. Moloo also uses task and timing information for cleaning sessions. Your device stores app settings and data needed to display the app.

Optional notifications

If you enable notifications, Moloo uses your device’s push token, reminder settings, time zone, task deadlines, task views, last visits when comeback reminders are enabled, and notification delivery records to send reminders and avoid duplicates. Apple delivers push notifications. You can change reminder settings in Moloo or disable notifications in iPhone Settings. Your iPhone’s preview settings control what others can see on your lock screen.

Purchases, sign-in and service providers

Apple processes purchases. Moloo uses purchase identifiers and access status to check coverage for your household. RevenueCat supplies purchase offers and processes purchase records with your Moloo app user identifier. Supabase provides account authentication, data storage and syncing. Apple or Google processes sign-in when you choose its service. If you email support, we use your email address and message to answer your request.

App usage measurement

Moloo sends onboarding and purchase-flow events to PostHog using a random session identifier, an event time, and the step or plan when relevant. In configured iPhone builds, AppsFlyer measures app activation after adult admission and receives a verified purchase event with the plan. Moloo disables these measurement paths for the managed child experience. For questions about service providers, contact support.

Optional Apple subscription refund reviews

In Moloo’s account options, open Refund privacy to choose whether Moloo may share purchase delivery and consumption information through RevenueCat with Apple when you request a subscription refund. Sharing starts off. If you allow it, Moloo recommends declining the refund and Apple decides. The choice applies to your own subscription. Your purchase and household access work either way.

Moloo records your choice, its date and the version of the explanation for your account. Choose Withdraw consent on the same screen to stop sharing for future requests. Withdrawal cannot recall information already sent to Apple.

Why we use data and how long we keep it

We use account, household, task and purchase information to provide the service you request. We use technical records to keep accounts secure, maintain the service and investigate problems. We process information when required by law. Your account remains available after subscription expiry. The rules below apply to tasks and their completion history. Support messages are kept as needed to resolve the issue and handle follow-up. Information subject to a legal retention requirement is kept for that purpose.

Task retention after paid access ends

Shared tasks and their completion history are locked at household expiry and scheduled for deletion after 30 days without household coverage. Each account has a separate 30-day period for private tasks and their completion history, starting when all paid access for that account ends. A member’s own valid purchase or another paying household can protect their private tasks. A parent cannot see a child’s private tasks.

We store the coverage end, deletion deadline and notice status. Seven days before shared deletion we provide a notice to the owner in Moloo. When available, we use an account’s registered device token, language and time zone to send a service notification with the deadline. Personal deletion dates are shown only to the affected account. A delay in making the in-app notice available extends the deadline to allow seven days to act. Restored coverage before deletion cancels that deletion. The account survives expiry. Renewing after deletion cannot restore deleted tasks. The deletion deadline refers to active service records. Separate backup rotation and any legally required records follow their own retention criteria.

På norsk · Lagring etter utløp

Fellesoppgaver og fullføringshistorikk sperres ved utløp og slettes etter 30 dager uten husstandsdekning. Hver konto har en egen 30-dagersfrist for private oppgaver og tilhørende historikk fra all betalt tilgang opphører. Gyldig eget kjøp eller dekning gjennom en betalt husstand kan sikre fortsatt personlig bruk. Foresatte får ikke innsyn i barnets private oppgaver.

Vi lagrer tidspunkt for utløp, slettefrist og varselstatus. Eieren får beskjed i Moloo sju dager før sletting av fellesoppgaver. Vi bruker enhetstoken, språk og tidssone til et tjenestevarsel når en registrert enhet er tilgjengelig. Den personlige slettefristen vises bare til kontoen den gjelder. Forsinket beskjed i appen gir minst sju dager til å reagere. Ny dekning før sletting avbryter slettingen. Kontoen består etter utløp. Slettede oppgaver kan ikke gjenopprettes ved fornyelse. Fristen gjelder aktive tjenestedata. Sikkerhetskopier og opplysninger som må beholdes etter loven, har egne slettekriterier.

Moloo terms of service · Bruksvilkår

Deletion and your choices

You can separately request account deletion from the account menu, including after paid coverage ends, or contact support. Follow the confirmation steps shown in your version of Moloo. Apple handles subscription renewal separately. You can manage your subscription here.

Depending on where you live, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your data, or object to certain uses. Email support to exercise these rights or ask about service providers and international processing. You can also contact your local data protection authority or Norway’s Datatilsynet.
