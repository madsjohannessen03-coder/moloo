Moloo privacy policy

Shared chores are visible to your household. Personal chores and their completion history are visible only to you in the app.

24 November 2026

Who handles your information

Mads Johannessen, Norway, is the data controller for Moloo. For questions about how Moloo handles your information or to exercise your rights, email support@mjappdev.com.

Your account and household

Moloo uses an account identifier, your sign-in provider’s identifier and the name or email supplied during sign-in. We store the profile name and avatar you choose, household membership, invitations and any username you make searchable so people can invite you. Share your household invite link or code only with people you want to invite.

Tasks and progress

We store task names, schedules, assignments, completion times and who completed a shared task. This gives your household a common calendar, a Clean score and a streak. Assigning a shared task to one person keeps it visible to the household. Moloo also uses task and timing information for cleaning sessions. Your device stores app settings and data needed to display the app.

Optional notifications

If you enable notifications, Moloo uses your device’s push token, reminder settings, time zone, task deadlines and notification delivery records to send reminders and avoid duplicates. Apple delivers push notifications. You can change reminder settings in Moloo or disable notifications in iPhone Settings. Your iPhone’s preview settings control what others can see on your lock screen.

Purchases, sign-in and service providers

Apple processes purchases. Moloo uses purchase identifiers and access status to check coverage for your household. RevenueCat helps manage purchase access and records when a purchase offer is displayed, together with the offer and app user identifiers. Supabase provides account authentication, data storage and syncing. Apple or Google processes sign-in when you choose its service. If you email support, we use your email address and message to answer your request.

Optional Apple subscription refund reviews

In Moloo’s account options, open Refund privacy to choose whether Moloo may share purchase delivery and consumption information through RevenueCat with Apple when you request a subscription refund. Sharing starts off. If you allow it, Moloo recommends declining the refund and Apple decides. The choice applies to your own subscription. Your purchase and household access work either way.

Moloo records your choice, its date and the version of the explanation for your account. Choose Withdraw consent on the same screen to stop sharing for future requests. Withdrawal cannot recall information already sent to Apple.

Why we use data and how long we keep it

We use account, household, task and purchase information to provide the service you request. We use technical records to keep accounts secure, maintain the service and investigate problems. We process information when required by law. Account and task records are kept while needed to provide your account and its history. Support messages are kept as needed to resolve the issue and handle follow-up. Information subject to a legal retention requirement is kept for that purpose.

Deletion and your choices

Choose Delete account in Moloo and confirm to start a manual deletion request. The app records the request time. Our target is to complete deletion within one month. Apple handles subscription renewal separately. You can manage your subscription here.

Depending on where you live, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your data, or object to certain uses. Email support to exercise these rights or ask about service providers and international processing. You can also contact your local data protection authority or Norway’s Datatilsynet.
